Case studies/Automotive · Gen AI

Multi-agent Gen AI platform for automotive service assistance

A production multi-agent, RAG-based assistant that helps service technicians with parts, diagnostics, labour times, tools, bulletins, manuals, training and symptoms, all from one conversation.

Role

Senior AI Architect, end to end

Cloud

Google Cloud Platform

Models

Gemini

Orchestration

LangGraph · FastAPI

A global automotive manufacturer, via a specialist Gen AI consultancy

01 · Context

The situation

Service technicians rely on many separate sources: parts catalogues, diagnostic trouble codes, flat-rate labour times, special-tool lists, service bulletins, shop manuals and training videos. Finding the right answer for a vehicle in the workshop meant searching several of them.

02 · Challenge

What had to be true

Bring all of these into one assistant that answers accurately and quickly at a controlled cost, with the security, governance and observability a global manufacturer requires.

Modules

Eight service-assistance modules

PartsDiagnostics / DTCFlat RateSpecial ToolsService BulletinsShop ManualTraining VideoSymptom-Based Assistance
03 · What we built

The system

  • End-to-end AI architecture for a multi-agent RAG platform on Google Cloud
  • Eight service-assistance modules, each with its own retrieval and answer logic
  • Retrieval engineering: hybrid search across PostgreSQL / Cloud SQL (pgvector, pg_trgm), OpenSearch and Vertex AI Vector Search
  • Agents in Python, FastAPI and LangGraph on Gemini models, with Gemini embeddings for retrieval
  • Performance, model and cost optimisation, with Redis caching
  • Evaluation and KPIs, plus observability in Grafana and BigQuery
  • Security, deployment governance and workshop validation with service teams
  • Stakeholder management across business and engineering teams
04 Architecture

How it works, step by step.

Step through the system, or let it play.

  1. A technician asks a question
  2. The orchestrator routes the intent
  3. Specialist modules take the task
  4. Hybrid retrieval grounds the answer
  5. Gemini writes a grounded answer
  6. Evaluated, logged and observed
05 · Outcome

Service teams get parts, diagnostics, repair times and procedures from one grounded assistant instead of eight separate sources.
