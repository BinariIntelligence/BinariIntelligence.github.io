Shopify store for organic spices and oils

A Shopify website to showcase and sell organic spices and extracted oils, with all imagery and video created using AI.

Platform

Shopify

Media

AI-generated images and video

Content

Product catalogue and blog

Operations

Inventory and order processing

An organic spice and extracted-oil brand

01 · Context

The situation

The brand needed an online store that explained its products as well as it sold them.

02 · Challenge

What had to be true

Launch a polished store with rich product media and educational content, without a photo shoot.

03 · What we built

The system

  • A responsive storefront with simple navigation
  • Product listings with descriptions, pricing and high-resolution images
  • Shopify integration for inventory, orders and checkout
  • A blog that explains the benefits of organic spices and oils
  • All images and videos created with AI for a consistent, modern look
04 Architecture

How it works, step by step.

Step through the system, or let it play.

  1. Catalogue and content planned
  2. AI images and video generated
  3. Store designed and built
  4. Products, inventory and checkout set up
  5. Blog launched
05 · Outcome

A modern store that sells and educates, launched without traditional product photography.
