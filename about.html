About Binari

An AI engineering studio for production systems.

Binari Intelligence designs and builds Gen AI, machine learning, NLP and computer vision systems, and the software around them, for companies that need AI to work every day, not just in a demo.

Who we are

Architecture-led. Delivery-focused.

Binari Intelligence System Private Limited was founded in July 2022. We work remotely with clients globally.

We take AI from idea to production: we choose the right first use case, design the system, build it with your team, and hand over something you can run and own.

Our work spans multi-agent Gen AI platforms, RAG assistants, document intelligence, adaptive testing, clinical NLP and real-time computer vision on edge devices, plus the web, mobile and cloud software that puts them in people's hands.

Binari Intelligence was founded by Roshini Kesavan.
