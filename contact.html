FAQ

Questions we hear first.

Where do the models run, and who sees our data?+

In your cloud account (Google Cloud, AWS or Azure), with your keys. We design so your data is not used to train third-party models.

Who owns the code and the models?+

You do. We hand over the code, prompts, evaluation suites and documentation.

How do you make Gen AI reliable enough for production?+

Evaluation suites built from your real cases, guardrails on inputs and actions, human approval where it matters, and monitoring after launch.

Do you work with teams outside India?+

Yes. We are remote-first and work with clients globally, with overlap hours agreed at the start.

Fixed price or time and materials?+

Architecture reviews are fixed scope. Build sprints and dedicated teams are planned together, with clear milestones.
